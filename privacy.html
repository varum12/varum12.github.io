Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

1. Who we are

Varum Video Uploader ("the App") is a personal desktop tool operated by its owner ("the Operator"). It is used only by the Operator, for the Operator's own TikTok accounts. The App has no other users.

2. What data the App handles

  • TikTok access tokens. When the Operator signs in with TikTok, the App receives an access token and a refresh token for the Operator's own accounts.
  • Basic account information. The nickname and the available privacy settings of the Operator's own accounts, used to display and validate a post before it is published.
  • Video files and post details. The videos the Operator creates, together with their titles and privacy settings.

The App does not collect data about any other person, and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking services.

3. Where data is stored

All data stays on the Operator's own computer. Access tokens are stored in a local file on that computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than TikTok's own APIs, which are used to upload the videos.

4. Sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties. Data is transmitted only to TikTok in order to publish the Operator's videos.

5. Retention and deletion

Access tokens are kept until they expire or until the Operator deletes the local token file. Access can also be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions. Video files are stored locally and can be deleted by the Operator at any time.

6. Security

Data is held on the Operator's own computer, protected by the operating system's user account. No public server stores any data from the App.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children and is not available to anyone other than the Operator.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. The current version is always available on this page.
