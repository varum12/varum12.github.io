Varum Video Uploader

A personal desktop tool that helps a single creator prepare and upload their own short videos to their own TikTok accounts.

What it does

  • Takes long-form videos the creator has recorded or licensed and splits them into short clips.
  • Combines the clips with the creator's own overlay footage and their own advertising clip.
  • Lets the creator review the finished clips before anything is published.
  • Uploads the approved clips to the creator's own TikTok accounts after the creator signs in with TikTok and confirms each post's title and privacy setting.

Who it is for

This tool is used only by its owner for their own TikTok accounts. It is not offered to other users, and it does not read or publish content belonging to anyone else.
